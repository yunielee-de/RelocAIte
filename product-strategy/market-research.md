# Anspruch — Market & Competitor Research (2026-09-10)

Source: based on the 9/9 pivot version of the [AI.WOMEN Hackathon — Business Plan Option 2 & Prep](https://app.notion.com/p/3d391f016bb981b0a34eeaea429a8b56) Notion page. Only data verified/supplemented via web search is compiled here. Figures already present in the Notion doc are re-cited with the original document's own sourcing.

## 1. Market size (for Slide 4)

- **Foreign workers in Germany: 6.53 million as of end-2025** (16.7% of the total 39.17M employed, more than double the 2010 share). Of these, 5.8 million are in social-insurance-contributing employment (as of Aug 2025) — 2.51M EU citizens + 3.31M non-EU.
  — [Mediendienst Integration](https://mediendienst-integration.de/arbeitsmarkt/auslaendische-arbeitskraefte/wie-viele-auslaender-arbeiten-in-deutschland/)
  → **Candidate SAM population = 6.53 million** (the 5.8M social-insurance-contributing subset is the more conservative baseline)
- **Average first-year tax refund for foreigners: €800–1,500** (higher than the general average of €1,095–1,172 — because full relocation-cost deductions are typically the largest single line item in the first year). Typical total deductible relocation costs: €3,000–6,000.
  — [Restio Blog](https://restio.io/de/blog/erste-steuererklaerung-expat), [VLH](https://www.vlh.de/wissen-service/steuer-nachrichten/durchschnittliche-steuererstattung-steigt-stetig-an.html)
- **Relocation cost flat-rate deduction (Umzugskostenpauschale) 2026: €964 for the individual + €643 per accompanying family member.** Also applies to international moves, though it may be limited under double-taxation agreements (DBA).
  — [Lohnsteuer-kompakt](https://www.lohnsteuer-kompakt.de/steuerwissen/erhoehung-der-umzugskostenpauschale/)
- **Homeoffice-Pauschale: €6/day, up to €1,260/year (210 days).** Starting 2026, the tax office has begun strictly requiring day-by-day documentation — which can be read as a signal that the number of people missing out on this simply because they don't know about it will grow. Could not find official statistics on what percentage actually claim it (recommend labeling as "unquantified, circumstantial" on the slide if needed).
  — [ad-hoc-news](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/homeoffice-pauschale-finanzaemter-verschaerfen-pruefung-ab-2026/68454970)
- **Bildungsurlaub (Hessen): 5 paid days/year, requires 6+ months of employment, unused days lapse at year-end in principle** (rollover possible by exception, but requires justification). Could not find actual usage-rate statistics — this part should be carried by personal testimony (Slide 2 Hook) rather than data.
  — [dein-bildungsurlaub.de](https://www.dein-bildungsurlaub.de/bundeslander/hessen), [wirsindderwandel.de](https://wirsindderwandel.de/recht/bildungsurlaub-wann-er-verfaellt-und-wie-sie-ihn-retten/)

**Assessment**: The tax/relocation-deduction figures are solid. Bildungsurlaub/Homeoffice-Pauschale usage rates have no available statistics, so the "losing out without knowing" framing needs to be carried by interview evidence (the Day 1–2 validation questions in the plan doc), not quantitative data — this is a real gap research can't fill, which makes the interviews more important.

## 2. Competitor re-verification (for Slide 8)

| Competitor | Actual coverage | Overlaps with this niche? |
|---|---|---|
| **Taxfix** | Tax filing only. 5M+ downloads, cumulative refunds €3–4.5bn+ (varies by source, most recent figure €4.5bn). English UI supported, also used by expats | 🟡 Partial — overlaps on tax, but doesn't cover non-tax rights like Bildungsurlaub / Homeoffice-Pauschale |
| **Sozialplattform.de** | Government-integrated platform. 10 application services + 3 counseling services (Geringverdiener-Pass, Seniorenpass, etc.), multilingual. **Explicitly targets low-income (Geringverdiener)** | 🟢 No overlap — different segment (confirmed) |
| **LeistungsLotse** (founded 2023, Poschenrieder & Becker) | Centered on Wohngeld/Bürgergeld/Grundsicherung. Targets renters, single parents, pension recipients (95% Grundsicherung). Problem framing is the 35–60% non-application rate | 🟢 No overlap — low-income only, confirmed |
| **Wundertax / Smartsteuer** | Same category as Taxfix (English-supported tax-filing apps) | 🟡 Same gap as Taxfix |
| **General relocation services** (Get In Expat, Expatrio, IamExpat) | Visa/housing/logistics-focused relocation services. Don't handle financial-entitlement discovery | 🟢 No overlap |
| **Existing startups named/conceptually similar to "Anspruch"** | Not found in search (not on German legal-tech lists either — Anwalt Guru, Noxtua etc. are general legal-advice AI, not entitlement-scanning tools) | — Low name-collision risk |
| A tool that scans tax + labor rights (Bildungsurlaub etc.) + deductions in one unified pass, per person | Not found in either English or German searches | **Appears to be a blank space — though "couldn't find it" isn't proof it doesn't exist. Needs final confirmation via the Day 3 plan of directly using Taxfix/Sozialplattform** |

**Conclusion**: The 9/9 pivot doc's claim that "our seat: nobody integrates tax + labor rights + subsidies on a per-person basis" is not overturned by this research round. However, confidence is at the "not 100% since we haven't used them hands-on" level — the doc's Day 3 plan (hands-on use of Taxfix and Sozialplattform, 30 min each) remains valid and should not be skipped.
