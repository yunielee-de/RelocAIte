ANSPRUCH§ 03 / 13 — Problem
Problem

Nothing pays you automatically.

  1. §1Every entitlement requires an application — silence gets you nothing.
  2. §2Backdating is narrow. Kindergeld: 6 months. Bürgergeld: none. Miss the window, lose the money.
  3. §3The information exists — in Amtsdeutsch, the dense bureaucratic German even natives struggle to parse.

If you don't know the term for what you're owed, you can't search for it.

Market Size

A blind spot the size of a country's workforce.

800–1.500 €average first-year tax refund for newcomers
964 €relocation cost flat-rate deduction (+643 € per family member)
1.260 €maximum annual Homeoffice-Pauschale

This isn't new. Germany already sits on money it's designed to pay out — and doesn't.

40% claim it
60% never do
Households claiming Grundsicherung im Alter Eligible households that never apply — ≈ €2bn stays unpaid, every year

Old-age basic security (Grundsicherung im Alter): ~625,000 eligible households never apply, most often because they don't know they qualify. Different benefit, same country, same failure mode — nothing says the state chases you down for your Bildungsurlaub or your tax refund any harder.
