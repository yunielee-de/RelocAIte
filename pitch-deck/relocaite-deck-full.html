Customer Journey

Moving to Germany is not one task. It is five opaque systems.

A foreign professional must manage a contract, visa, housing, insurance, and tax/rights — across five separate systems, five separate deadlines.

Offer “Does this contract meet the visa threshold?” 2026 Blue Card: €50,700 May miss eligibility entirely.
Visa “Is this offer visa-ready?” D-Visa docs, embassy lead time Can lose weeks to one document.
Home “Can I register at this address?” Anmeldung within 14 days Can lose bank account access.
Insurance “Which insurance is necessary?” Public vs. private, before Anmeldung May miss a hard-to-reverse window.
Rights & Tax “What can I still claim?” Bildungsurlaub, Homeoffice, relocation Can lose paid days that expire.
Eligibility decisions remain with the relevant authority or licensed adviser.
